# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to keep this repository professional, respectful, and useful for everyone who works on it.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Focus feedback on code, design, content, and product decisions.
- Respect studio and client privacy.
- Keep discussions free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and doxxing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or abusive language.
- Sexual, violent, or hateful content.
- Sharing private contact details, unpublished project material, or other confidential business information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit, hide, lock, or remove participation from discussions that violate this code of conduct.

## Contact

For conduct concerns, contact the project owner through the repository contact details in [README.md](README.md).
